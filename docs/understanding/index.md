# Understanding BenchRep

Explore how BenchRep interprets configurations, builds models, runs workflows, and records their results. The sections below explain the shared rules and the behavior specific to each workflow. For runnable examples, see [Tutorials](../guides/index.md).

- **[Workflows & configuration](workflows/index.md):** How workflow inputs are assembled, interpreted, and edited.
- **[Data](data/index.md):** How datasets, transforms, and datamodules supply data to a workflow.
- **[Models & objectives](models/index.md):** How canonical and composite models connect components and learning objectives.
- **[Training](training/index.md):** How training is controlled, monitored, and recorded.
- **[Prediction](prediction/index.md):** How training runs become inference inputs and exported representations.
- **[Evaluation](evaluation/index.md):** How model outputs are analyzed, scored, and visualized.
- **[Results & provenance](results/index.md):** Where outputs live, what their records mean, and how to interpret a run.
- **[Extensibility](extensibility/index.md):** How to discover registered components and supply your own implementations.
- **[Execution internals](internals/index.md):** How BenchRep implements workflows and the wizard, and where to find the relevant code.
